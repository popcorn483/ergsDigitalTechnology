Mark in Claude

Marks pupils’ past-paper pages in the Claude app on your Max plan, with no API key. Pick the paper, take photos of the pages, and send them to Claude with that paper’s official mark schemes attached. Claude replies with the marks for each question, page by page.

1Which paper?

2Photograph the page

One page at a time works best: flat, well lit, the whole page in the picture. You can add more than one page.

3Send to Claude

Choose Claude in the share menu. The photo and the mark schemes go into a new chat, and Claude marks the page.

2Mark it in your SCHOOL project

Your SCHOOL project in Claude holds the mark schemes for every past-paper question on the site, so Claude can work out which paper and questions a page is from.

  1. Open your SCHOOL project in the Claude app (button below, or Projects › SCHOOL).
  2. Start a new chat and tap the camera to photograph the page.
  3. Send it with mark this page. Claude finds the paper and questions, tells you which it used, and marks each answer.
Open SCHOOL project in Claude

If it picks the wrong paper, tell it (e.g. “this is Unit 1 Summer 2018”). Once you know the paper, choosing it above is quicker and works in any chat.

If Claude doesn’t appear in the share menu

Install the Claude app and sign in. If the share menu still doesn’t offer it, or the mark schemes don’t come through with the photo: press Copy mark schemes only, open the Claude app, start a chat (your SCHOOL project is ideal), paste, and attach the photo.

On a computer, Send to Claude copies the mark schemes, saves the photo and opens claude.ai: paste and attach the photo there.

Photos go to Claude to be marked; keep pupils’ names and candidate numbers out of the picture. Claude’s marks are suggestions: you decide the final mark.
