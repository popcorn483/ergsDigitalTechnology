    Examiner reports

    How to add a report
    • Upload CCEA’s Chief Examiner’s report PDF to papers/GCSE/Examiner reports/ or papers/A level/Examiner reports/ in the GitHub repo, named after the series, e.g. Summer 2027.pdf. Any name with the year works.
    • A GitHub Action finds each question’s comment (the unit’s section, then “Q3”, “(b)”, “(ii)”) a minute or two later.
    • Where a report discusses a whole question in one paragraph, the pack shows that paragraph with the lines about the part highlighted.
    • CCEA publishes no report for the 2021 series, the specimen papers or the 2022 A2 1 resit, so those questions have no report section.

    The papers, mark schemes and examiner reports are CCEA material, used with permission and shown unchanged. Every page of a pack says so. Packs are made in your browser from the PDFs in this site; nothing is uploaded.
